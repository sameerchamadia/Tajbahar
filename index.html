Our Heritage

Rooted in the
Soil of Emperors

Taj Bahar was born from a conviction: that the world's finest rice deserves to be grown where it has always thrived — in the mineral-rich alluvial plains of northern India, watered by rivers older than memory.

Our name echoes the Persian for "crown of spring" — a tribute to the Mughal courts where fragrant basmati rice was considered a gift worthy of royalty. We carry that tradition forward, from field to freight, with uncompromising care.

Our Process

From Sacred Fields
to Your Table

Every sack of Taj Bahar rice carries the story of a precise, unhurried journey — from hand-selected paddy to laboratory-certified perfection.

01

Precision Sourcing

We partner exclusively with certified farmers in the Haryana and Punjab belts — the only regions where true basmati can grow. Every crop season is personally supervised.

02

Controlled Ageing

Raw paddy is aged in our climate-controlled silos for 18 to 24 months. This intensifies the signature aroma and ensures grains expand fully upon cooking.

03

Multi-Stage Milling

State-of-the-art sortex and colour-sorting machines remove any impurity. Our zero-breakage policy ensures you receive only whole, pristine grains.

04

Lab Certification & Packing

Every batch is tested for moisture content, grain length, aroma grade, and pesticide residue before hermetically sealed packaging for preservation.
